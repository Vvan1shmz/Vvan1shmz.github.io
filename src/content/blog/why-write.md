---
title: why write things down
date: 2026-09-07
description: Writing is how a thought leaves your head and becomes something you can check.
---

ideas change shape while they sit in your head. a few days later it is hard to tell which sentence you actually believed and which one was just a mood.

once it is written, it stays. you can edit it, argue with it, or come back months later and notice you were wrong. that is more honest than letting it spin forever.

so this blog is not for a posting schedule, and it is not for being discovered. it is first a place for me.
